## A Global Surfacing Brand, Not Just a Colour Catalogue

Greenlam is among the world's top 3 and Asia's largest surfacing solutions brands, with a presence in over 120 countries. Every Greenlam laminate carries the brand's baseline protections — anti-virus and anti-bacterial treatment (retarding up to 99.9% of common bacteria and viruses, including SARS-CoV-2), fire-retardant testing, NSF food-safety certification, and a 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects. That's before you even get to the design.

Like most large laminate manufacturers, Greenlam treats finish as a genuine design variable rather than a fixed property of a shade — the same decor code can ship in several distinct textures, each changing how the surface looks, feels, and wears day to day.

## Everyday Finishes

The core range most Greenlam shades are available in:

- **Suede (SUD)** — a soft, matte, low-reflectivity finish. The most common choice for high-touch surfaces since it hides fingerprints, smudges and daily wear well.
- **Super Gloss (SGL)** — a highly reflective, mirror-like finish for a formal, premium look. Shows fingerprints and scratches more readily than matte finishes.
- **Satin (SAT)** — a silky, super-matte finish that sits between Suede and full gloss — soft to the touch with a refined, understated sheen.
- **HD Gloss (HDG)** — Greenlam's "High Definition Gloss," engineered specifically to resist marring, scratches and abrasion better than standard gloss laminates, without giving up the reflective, shiny look.
- **Jupiter (JUP)** — a stone-textured finish, typically paired with marble and travertine-style patterns for a tactile, natural-stone feel.
- **Fawn (FWN)** — a soft, muted matte finish, often used on pastel and neutral solid colours.
- **Olmo (OLM)** — a fine, subtly textured matte finish.
- **Edgy Ash (ASH)** — a textured matte finish with a slightly rougher, more organic hand-feel than standard Suede.

## Specialty Collections

Greenlam's "Specials" range — distinct textures built around a specific material or performance story, not just a surface sheen:

- **Aran (ARN)** — a woven textile texture with a gently irregular, handwoven-fabric pattern. Adds softness and warmth to wardrobes, wall panelling and premium furniture.
- **Barrel (BRL)** — a sophisticated woodgrain texture with a soft, harmonious structure, designed for a calm, organic look.
- **Canyon (CNY)** — a bold, large-scale stone texture with dramatic depth and contrast, built to enhance both stone décors and solid-colour surfaces.
- **VRB** — the finest expression of real wood veneer laid onto laminate, giving a dry, matte finish that reads as genuine wood veneer rather than a printed pattern.
- **AFX** — Greenlam's anti-fingerprint laminate: an ultra-matte, scratch-resistant, hydro-repellent surface that wipes clean of grease and impurities without showing fingerprint smudges. Also mould-resistant and hygienic.
- **Unicore** — laminates coloured all the way through their core, not just on the surface, in HD Gloss, Super Gloss or Suede textures. Sturdier and more durable, with no dark edge line at cut corners — the practical choice for cabinets, countertops and shelving that see heavy edge wear.
- **Brushed foils** — metallic finishes (silver, gold, copper, bronze, steel) with a brushed, directional texture, recommended for vertical applications like feature panels.

## How to Actually Use This

Match the finish to how the surface gets used, not just how it looks in a sample:

- **Kitchen shutters and high-touch surfaces** — Suede, Satin or AFX hide daily wear and fingerprints far better than gloss finishes.
- **Feature walls and low-touch accent panels** — HD Gloss, Super Gloss or Canyon's stone drama read as premium without the upkeep concern of a high-touch surface.
- **Cabinet edges and countertops with heavy wear** — Unicore's colour-through-core construction avoids the dark edge line other laminates show at cut corners, and holds up better to chipping.
- **Wardrobe interiors and low-visibility panels** — Aran's woven texture or Barrel's woodgrain add tactile quality where gloss level matters less.
