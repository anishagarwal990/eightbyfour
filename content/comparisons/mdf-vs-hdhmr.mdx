## Spec Comparison

| | MDF | HDHMR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Density | Medium | High |
| Moisture resistance | Low — dry interior use only | High — tolerates humidity and occasional moisture |
| Surface finish | Smooth, machines and paints cleanly | Smooth, machines cleanly, holds detail well for profile routing |
| Typical cost | Lower | Higher |
| Typical use | Painted panelling, false ceiling trims, dry furniture | Wardrobe shutters, kitchen-adjacent cabinetry, profile-routed shutters |

## The Short Version

Choose MDF where the application stays fully dry and cost matters more than moisture tolerance. Choose HDHMR wherever the board might see humidity, splashing, or is going into a wardrobe or cabinet near a wet area — the extra cost buys real moisture resistance MDF doesn't have.
