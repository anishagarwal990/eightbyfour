## Fit-Outs Governed by Compliance, Not Just Design

Commercial fit-outs — offices, co-working spaces, business centres — usually answer to a building fire code and a facilities-management durability expectation, on top of the design brief. Material grade decisions here are often compliance-driven rather than purely aesthetic.

## Recommended Products

- **Structural joinery and partitions:** Fire-retardant grade plywood — see [Plywood](/products/plywood).
- **Surfaces:** Laminate for desks, partitions and storage — see [Laminates](/products/laminates).
- **Feature areas:** Stone panels for reception and common areas — see [Stone Panels](/products/stone-panels).
- **Bonding:** Fevicol adhesive range — see [Adhesives](/products/adhesive).

## Recommended Brands

Century and Green Panel both stock plywood suited to commercial-grade specification; Fevicol covers the adhesive side of the BOQ.

## Buying Tips

Get your fire safety and building compliance grade requirement confirmed in writing before finalising plywood grade — it's cheaper to spec correctly once than to re-do joinery after a compliance review.
