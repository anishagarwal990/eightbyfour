## Fit-Outs That Need to Read Premium and Perform Commercially

Hotel interiors carry two demands most residential projects don't: a consistent premium finish across dozens or hundreds of repeated units (rooms, corridors), and commercial-grade durability under daily guest and housekeeping use.

## Recommended Products

- **Structural joinery:** Fire-retardant grade plywood — see [Plywood](/products/plywood).
- **Feature walls and reception:** Natural or dyed veneer, stone panels — see [Veneers](/products/veneers) and [Stone Panels](/products/stone-panels).
- **Counters and wet areas:** Acrylic solid surface — see [Corian / Acrylic Solid Surface](/products/corian-acrylic-solid-surface).

## Recommended Brands

Our EightxFour curated veneer and stone panel line covers most hospitality feature-wall specs; Durasein and Tiara solid surface ranges suit reception counters and wash areas.

## Buying Tips

For repeated rooms, lock the veneer or laminate batch/flitch at project start and order the full quantity upfront — sourcing a matching batch mid-project after running out is the single most common hospitality fit-out delay.
