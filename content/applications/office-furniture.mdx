## Furniture That Sees Daily Commercial Wear

Office furniture takes more daily handling than most residential furniture — drawers open dozens of times a day, desk edges take constant contact. Durability and edge finish quality matter more here than in a home study.

## Recommended Products

- **Desks and workstation carcasses:** 18–19mm commercial plywood — see [Plywood](/products/plywood).
- **Exposed-edge shelving or reception furniture:** Birch plywood for a clean, finished edge — see [Birch Plywood](/products/birch-plywood).
- **Surface finish:** Laminate for desks and storage fronts, or veneer for reception and premium areas — see [Laminates](/products/laminates) and [Veneers](/products/veneers).

## Recommended Brands

Century and Greenply plywood suit standard workstation and storage builds; our Russian Birch Ply stock is worth specifying wherever a shelf or panel edge will be visible.

## Buying Tips

For open-plan offices with modular workstations, standardise on one plywood thickness and one laminate finish across all desks — it simplifies future repairs and reordering compared to mixing specs desk by desk.
