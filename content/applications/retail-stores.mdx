## Fit-Outs Built Around a Fixed Opening Date

Retail fit-outs live and die by their opening date — the material choice matters, but availability and lead time often matter just as much. EightxFour's direct manufacturer relationships are built for exactly this: confirming real stock fast rather than quoting against a catalogue that may not be in Hyderabad.

## Recommended Products

- **Fixtures and display units:** Commercial plywood with laminate finish — see [Plywood](/products/plywood) and [Laminates](/products/laminates).
- **Feature walls and counters:** Veneer or acrylic solid surface — see [Veneers](/products/veneers) and [Solid Surface / Corian](/products/corian-acrylic-solid-surface).

## Recommended Brands

Greenply plywood and laminate cover most standard fixture builds; our EightxFour veneer and stone line suits feature walls and premium retail counters.

## Buying Tips

Confirm brand and finish availability before finalising design renders — swapping a specified finish mid-fit-out because of a stock gap is a common source of opening-date delay. Tell us your target date up front and we'll flag stock risk early.
