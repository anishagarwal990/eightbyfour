## A Feature Piece, Not Just Storage

TV units are usually the most visually prominent piece of joinery in a living room, which means finish quality tends to matter more here than raw structural grade — most TV units don't carry heavy load or see moisture, so the spec can lean toward appearance.

## Recommended Products

- **Panels and shelving:** 12–18mm plywood — see [Plywood](/products/plywood).
- **Finish:** Veneer for a real-wood look, or laminate for a wider colour range — see [Veneers](/products/veneers) and [Laminates](/products/laminates).
- **Bonding:** Standard wood adhesive — see [Adhesives](/products/adhesive).

## Recommended Brands

Century and Greenply plywood cover the panel and shelving base for most TV unit builds.

## Material Combinations

Floating TV units with backlit panels often pair veneer-faced plywood with a laminate or stone-panel accent strip — check our [Stone Panels](/products/stone-panels) category if you want a backlit or textured feature strip.

## Buying Tips

If the unit is wall-mounted or floating, confirm your fabricator's mounting bracket load rating against the panel thickness before finalising material — thinner panels need reinforcement at the mounting points.
