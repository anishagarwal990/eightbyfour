## Why EightByFour

Acrylic laminates give shutters and wardrobe fronts a mirror-gloss, glass-like finish at a fraction of the weight and cost of real glass or acrylic solid surface panelling — which is why they've become a default choice for modular kitchens and wardrobe fronts across Hyderabad projects. We stock the full Propperly acrylic range — our own curated line, sourced and quality-checked specifically for local fabrication — rather than a thin slice of one manufacturer's catalogue.

The Propperly acrylic range spans three finishes, all in 1.5mm sheets ready for bonding onto plywood or MDF:

- **[Acrylic Matt Collection](/products/laminates?collection=Acrylic%20Matt%20Collection)** — the same premium look with a soft-touch matte surface that resists fingerprints and fine scratches.

Together that's 59 shade options — enough range to match almost any modular kitchen or wardrobe colour scheme without waiting on a special order.
