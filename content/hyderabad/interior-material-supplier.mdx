## Why EightByFour

Most interior projects need materials from six or seven different categories — plywood, laminates, veneers, hardware, adhesives — each traditionally sourced from a different specialist supplier. EightByFour consolidates that into one procurement relationship for Hyderabad projects, so your BOQ doesn't turn into a dozen separate vendor conversations.

Our catalogue spans established manufacturers including Century, Greenply, Austin, Green Panel, Wigwam Excel, Durasein, Tiara, Fevicol and our own Propperly curated veneer and stone line.
