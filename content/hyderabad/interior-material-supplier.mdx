## Why EightByFour

The interior fit-out phase of a project runs on a different set of materials than the core build — plywood, laminates, veneers, hardware and adhesives for cabinetry, wardrobes and panelling, rather than structural or exterior materials. Interior designers, fit-out contractors and homeowners sourcing specifically for this phase don't need a general building-materials supplier; they need someone who stocks the finish-layer categories directly and can move fast once a spec is locked. That's the specific gap EightByFour fills.

## Brands Available

Our catalogue spans established manufacturers including Century, Greenply, Austin, Green Panel, Wigwam Excel, Durasein, Tiara, Fevicol and our own Propperly curated veneer and laminate lines.

## Delivery

We deliver across Hyderabad, coordinating multi-category orders (plywood, laminate, adhesive, hardware) as a single delivery where possible to simplify fit-out site logistics.

## Procurement Process

Share your interior fit-out BOQ or spec list — we'll consolidate stock confirmation and trade pricing across every finish-material category into a single quote rather than requiring separate inquiries per material.
