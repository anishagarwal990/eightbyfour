## Why EightByFour

A real project BOQ rarely stays within one material category — it's plywood and laminate and adhesive and hardware, all needed on the same timeline. This page is specifically about that document: send it as-is, in whatever format it already exists in, and we handle the line-by-line work of turning it into a priced, stock-confirmed order — no reformatting into our template, no re-keying quantities into five different supplier portals.

## Brands Available

Our BOQ fulfilment draws on established manufacturers across every category we stock — plywood, laminates, MDF/HDHMR, veneers, stone panels, acrylic solid surface, hardware and adhesives.

## Delivery

BOQ orders are scheduled against your site timeline, with items consolidated into as few deliveries as practical.

## Procurement Process

Send your BOQ — spreadsheet, PDF, or a plain list. We confirm stock and trade pricing line by line, flag anything with a longer lead time upfront, and coordinate delivery once you confirm the order.
