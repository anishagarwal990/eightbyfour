## Why EightByFour

A building project's material needs don't arrive in one neat order — they come in waves, as phases start, specs change, and quantities get revised against site progress. Chasing a fresh set of supplier quotes every time that happens is the real cost of fragmented procurement, more than any single order's pricing. EightByFour is built to be an ongoing procurement partner for the length of your project: one relationship, one point of contact, sourced directly from manufacturers, across every phase from first delivery to snagging-list touch-ups.

## Brands Available

We source across an established manufacturer network spanning plywood, laminates, MDF/HDHMR, veneers, stone panels, acrylic solid surface and adhesives.

## Delivery

Most in-stock items deliver same-day or next-day across Hyderabad. As a standing procurement relationship, we track what's already been delivered to your site, so follow-on orders coordinate against your existing schedule rather than starting from a blank slate.

## Procurement Process

1. Share your first BOQ or project brief — this becomes your project's baseline.
2. We check stock and trade pricing across our manufacturer network and return a consolidated quote.
3. As your project progresses, send follow-on requirements against the same project — no need to re-explain context each time.
4. Delivery is scheduled against your live site timeline for every order, not just the first.
