## Why EightByFour

Building material procurement in Hyderabad has traditionally meant relationship-by-relationship sourcing — a plywood dealer here, a hardware supplier there, a laminate distributor somewhere else, each with their own stock gaps and lead times. EightByFour exists to remove that fragmentation: one procurement relationship across all of it.

## Brands Available

We source across an established manufacturer network spanning plywood, laminates, MDF/HDHMR, veneers, stone panels, acrylic solid surface and adhesives.

## Delivery

Delivery is coordinated across Hyderabad against your site schedule, with multi-category BOQs consolidated where possible.

## Procurement Process

1. Share your BOQ or project brief.
2. We check stock and trade pricing across our distributor network.
3. You get a consolidated quote — not a patchwork of separate supplier quotes.
4. Delivery is scheduled against your site timeline.
