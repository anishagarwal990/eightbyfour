## Why EightByFour

Laminate finish is one of the most consequential — and most reversible-feeling until it isn't — decisions in an interior project. EightByFour's laminate catalogue spans the finishes Hyderabad fabricators actually stock and use, so what you spec is what you can get, on the timeline your project needs.

## Brands Available

Our laminate catalogue includes Greenply and other established manufacturers across matte, glossy, textured and wood-grain finishes.

## Delivery

Laminate orders are delivered across Hyderabad, typically within the week for in-stock finishes and thicknesses.

Share your shade codes or finish preferences, along with thickness and sheet count, and we'll confirm stock and pricing. If you haven't finalised finishes yet, we can help narrow the shortlist based on application (kitchen, wardrobe, retail).
