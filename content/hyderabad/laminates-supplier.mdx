## Why EightxFour

Laminate finish is one of the most consequential — and most reversible-feeling until it isn't — decisions in an interior project. EightxFour's laminate catalogue spans the finishes Hyderabad fabricators actually stock and use, so what you spec is what you can get, on the timeline your project needs.

## Brands Available

Greenlam, Merino, Virgo, Century Laminates and Sky Decor, across matte, high gloss, suede, textured and wood-grain finishes — plus acrylic and ASA sheets where a mirror finish is specified. Shade codes and finish codes are listed per sheet in the catalogue, which is what you need to quote from rather than a colour name.

## Delivery

Laminate orders are delivered across Hyderabad, typically within the week for in-stock finishes and thicknesses.

## Procurement Process

Share your shade codes or finish preferences, along with thickness and sheet count, and we'll confirm stock and pricing. If you haven't finalised finishes yet, we can help narrow the shortlist based on application (kitchen, wardrobe, retail).

Laminate is quoted per 8×4 ft sheet, and within a collection the rate is generally uniform across every shade in it.
