# Category fallback images

Real EightByFour product photos for categories with zero live SKUs yet (so the
homepage grid isn't a wall of grey boxes while catalogue is being built out).
Sourced from the repo's own `Product images /` and `Brand logo/` folders — not
stock photos. Move each into Supabase as a real product row once that
category is actually stocked, and this fallback stops being used automatically.
